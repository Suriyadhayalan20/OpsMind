# Car Insurance - Claim Policy (Sample)

## How to make a claim
1. Report the accident within 48 hours by phone or in the app.
2. Upload photos of the damage.
3. Upload your driving licence, RC book, and FIR copy (for theft or major accidents).
4. A surveyor will inspect the car within 3 working days.

## Deductible
The deductible is the amount you pay first. For standard plans it is Rs 2,000 per claim.

## Not covered
- Driving without a valid licence
- Driving under the influence of alcohol
- Normal wear and tear
