# Return and Refund Policy (Sample)

## Returns
- Most products can be returned within 7 days of delivery.
- The product must be unused and in the original box.
- Phones and laptops: return only if damaged or defective on arrival.

## Broken or damaged product
Upload a photo of the damage within 48 hours of delivery. We will send a replacement or a full refund.

## Refunds
- UPI / card: 5-7 working days after we receive the product.
- Cash on delivery: refund to bank account in 7-10 working days.
