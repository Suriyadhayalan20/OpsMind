# Leave Policy (Sample)

## Leave types per year
- Casual leave: 12 days
- Sick leave: 10 days (medical certificate needed for more than 2 days in a row)
- Earned leave: 15 days (can carry forward up to 30 days)

## How to apply
Apply in the HR portal at least 3 days before for casual leave. Your manager approves it.

## Notice period
- Probation: 30 days
- After confirmation: 60 days
